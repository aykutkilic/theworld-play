# Story foley

No audio files are used: the story foley (`src/ui/foley.ts`) synthesises its eight sounds (stamp, tick, flip, tear,
distant guns, peel, card) at play time from filtered noise and short sine thumps, with the Web Audio API. Nothing here
comes from anyone else, so there is nothing to license. If recorded CC0 sounds replace them later, list each file, its
source URL and its CC0 licence here.
